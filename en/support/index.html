Help

Answers to common questions. If something is missing, use our contact form or write to hallo@mondstunde.com

How does the free week work?

New subscribers can try Mondstunde free for 7 days. Cancel before the week ends and you pay nothing. After that, the subscription renews automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions run through your Apple account. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mondstunde, or in the app: Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I have a new iPhone. How do I get my subscription back?

In the app, open Settings → Restore purchases. Child profiles, stories and favorites come back automatically through your iCloud if you are signed in with the same Apple Account. AI voice audio is not transferred: if you listen to a story again, it is voiced again and counts towards your listening minutes. Without iCloud, your stories come along if you set up the new iPhone from a backup of the old one.

I deleted the app. Is my subscription gone?

No. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription; it keeps running through your Apple account. To cancel, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If you reinstall the app, tap Settings → Restore purchases. Your stories and profiles come back through iCloud; without iCloud, they are deleted together with the app.

How many stories can I create?

With a subscription, up to 21 new stories per week (fair use). You can read and listen to finished stories as often as you like.

What are listening minutes?

The AI voice costs us money per minute, so there is an allowance: 200 minutes with Family, 30 minutes with Basic, each per 4 weeks. Stories that were read aloud once are saved and cost no minutes when you listen again. You can see your balance in Settings.

Can I listen without internet?

Yes, once a story has been read aloud with the AI voice. New stories need an internet connection.

Does the story keep playing when I lock my phone?

Yes. The story and the sleep sound keep playing and can be controlled from the lock screen. Only swiping the app away in the app switcher stops playback.

Are the stories suitable for children?

Every story is checked automatically before it is shown: no violence, nothing scary, nobody is mocked. If anything still seems off, please let us know.

I have a code. Where do I redeem it?

In the app, go to Settings → Subscription → Redeem code, or tap “Redeem code” at the bottom of the plans screen. If you received a link with a code, just tap it.

Can I share a story with grandparents?

Yes. In a story, tap the share icon at the top: as a PDF to print, as an audiobook (if the story was read aloud) or as text.

What data does Mondstunde store?

You don't need an account. For a story we only send your child's first name, age and form of address, the options you chose, the names of other characters (with their role, e.g. “Rex (dog)”) and the favorite things you entered to Google Gemini. The app asks for your consent first. Stories and profiles are stored on your device and in your private iCloud, which we cannot access. Audio stays on the device. Settings → Delete all data removes your stories, profiles and anonymous account with one tap, including from iCloud. What is kept for a short time afterwards is explained in our privacy policy.

Can Mondstunde read aloud through a speaker in my child's room?

Yes. In the player, use the AirPlay icon to choose a HomePod, AirPlay or Bluetooth speaker. The story keeps playing even when the phone is locked or in another room. With AirPlay, the same Wi-Fi is enough; with Bluetooth, keep the phone nearby. Notification sounds stay on the phone.

Is there an Advent calendar?

Yes. From December 1 to 24, a new door opens every evening with a new story, and together they tell one tale leading up to Christmas Eve. You choose one of three stories, who brings the presents in your family (the Christkind, Santa Claus or no one in particular), who joins in and how long each evening is. A prologue is available from November, and missed evenings can be caught up. Each door counts as a new story.

Do I have to type in siblings and cuddly toys every time?

No. The app remembers them in the child profile under “Family & Friends”, with a role such as “sister” or “dog”. Under “Who's in the story?” you then just tap them. Siblings with their own profile show up there automatically.
