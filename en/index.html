What makes Mondstunde special

No off-the-shelf stories: each one is new, made for your evening.

Your child is the hero

With their name, siblings and cuddly toys. Or let other characters tell the story.

Made from your ideas

Pick a world and a goal, like space and building courage. Mondstunde writes a brand-new story from them every time.

Grows with your child

Language and plot fit the age, from 2 to 15. A slider makes stories simpler or richer.

Helps with falling asleep

Calm voices, rain, sea or campfire in the background, and sleep exercises like a breathing journey.

Stories continue

Any story can become a series. You decide what happens in tomorrow's adventure.

Safe and ad-free

Every story is checked to be child-friendly. No ads, no tracking, no account needed.
