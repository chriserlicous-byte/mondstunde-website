Questions

Common questions

How does the free week work?

New subscribers can try Mondstunde free for 7 days. Cancel before the week ends and you pay nothing. After that, the subscription renews automatically.

How many stories can I create?

With a subscription, up to 5 new stories per day (fair use). You can read and listen to finished stories as often as you like.

What are listening minutes?

The AI voice costs us money per minute, so there is an allowance: 200 minutes with Family, 30 minutes with Basic, each per 4 weeks. Stories that were read aloud once are saved and cost no minutes when you listen again. You can see your balance in Settings.

What data does Mondstunde store?

You don't need an account. For a story we only send your child's first name, age and form of address, the options you chose and the names of other characters. Stories and audio stay on your device. Settings → Delete all data removes everything with one tap.
