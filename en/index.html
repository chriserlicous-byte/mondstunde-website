Magic forest

Emma and the Cozy Forest Bed

5 min · Your own goal · for Emma

0:004:26

Emma walked through the soft green grass of the enchanted forest. Her plush toy bunny, Hoppy, rested safely under her arm. Her big brother, Ben, walked beside her along the winding path. Glowing blue flowers lit up the gentle evening air. The tall, friendly trees swayed softly in the light breeze. Everything felt quiet, peaceful, and warm.

“Look at the little moss nest,” Emma whispered to Ben. A tiny forest bird was tucking its wings into a green cushion. The bird fluttered softly and chirped a gentle goodnight song. Ben smiled and held Emma’s hand as they watched. “Every forest friend has a cozy sleeping spot,” Ben said gently. Emma hugged Hoppy tight and nodded her head.

A soft silver deer stepped out from behind a silver tree. It curled up all alone on a bed of dry leaves. The deer looked very safe and happy in its own little place. Emma watched the deer close its big, gentle eyes. “The deer likes its own bed,” Emma said softly. “It looks so peaceful there,” Ben agreed with a quiet smile.

Soon, Emma, Ben, and Hoppy walked back to their warm wooden cabin. The enchanted forest glowed softly under the rising silver moon. Stars began to blink through the branches like tiny nightlights. Emma felt her eyelids getting a little bit heavy. She held Hoppy close against her warm cotton pajamas. It was time to get ready for sleep.

Ben helped Emma climb into her very own big bed. Her blanket was soft and smelled like fresh lavender flowers. Her fluffy pillow was cool, smooth, and very inviting. “You have your own cozy nest, just like the deer,” Ben said. Emma tucked Hoppy beneath the warm blanket right next to her. She looked around her quiet, safe room.

Emma felt a little unsure about staying alone in her room. Ben sat on the edge of the mattress and smiled kindly. “I am just across the hall in my room,” Ben whispered. “And you have brave, soft Hoppy right beside you.” Emma touched Hoppy’s long, velvety ears. She realized her own bed was the coziest place in the world.

“I can do it,” Emma said in a proud, gentle whisper. “I can sleep in my own warm bed all night.” Ben gave her a soft kiss on her forehead. He turned on her little star lamp before walking to the door. “Good night, Emma, sleep well,” Ben whispered quietly. He left the door open just a little bit.

The room was peaceful, warm, and very quiet. Emma took a slow, deep breath in and let it out. She felt her feet relax under the heavy, cozy blanket. She felt her shoulders sink down into the soft mattress. Hoppy rested quietly against her arm, sharing the soft pillow. Emma smiled as she watched the gentle star lamp shine.

Outside the window, the enchanted forest rested under the silver moon. The little bird slept in the moss, and the deer slept on leaves. Emma felt safe, cozy, and very proud in her own bed. Her breathing became slow, deep, and steady like a soft song. She closed her eyes and drifted into sweet, happy dreams.

Three steps

How it works

From the first tap to falling asleep takes just a few minutes.

  1. 1

    Design the story

    Pick a world, a goal and a length. Siblings, friends, pets or a cuddly toy join in, each in their own role.

  2. 2

    Listen together

    A calm voice reads aloud, with rain, sea or campfire if you like. Even with the phone locked.

  3. 3

    Continue tomorrow

    Any story can become a series. The app suggests three ideas, or you bring your own.

Read aloud without a screen

The phone stays outside

Mondstunde can also read aloud through a speaker in your child's room, for example a HomePod mini. You start the story, and your child listens in the dark, without any screen.

  1. 1

    Choose a speaker

    In the player, tap the AirPlay icon and pick the HomePod or another speaker.

  2. 2

    Put the phone away

    The story keeps playing, even with the screen locked or from the next room. With AirPlay, the same Wi-Fi is all it takes.

  3. 3

    Only the story can be heard

    Notifications stay on the phone. When the story ends, the sleep sound fades out gently.

Works with any AirPlay or Bluetooth speaker. If you'd rather not have a microphone in your child's room, a simple Bluetooth speaker does the job.

Questions

Common questions

How does the free week work?

New subscribers can try Mondstunde free for 7 days. Cancel before the week ends and you pay nothing. After that, the subscription renews automatically.

Are the stories suitable for children?

Every story is checked automatically before it is shown: no violence, nothing scary, nobody is mocked. If anything still seems off, please let us know.

What are listening minutes?

The AI voice costs us money per minute, so there is an allowance: 200 minutes with Family, 30 minutes with Basic, each per 4 weeks. The minutes are deducted when a story with the AI voice is created. After that you can listen to it as often as you like, on your other devices too. You can see your balance in Settings.

What data does Mondstunde store?

You don't need an account. For a story we only send your child's first name, age and form of address, the options you chose, the names of other characters (with their role, e.g. “Rex (dog)”) and the favorite things you entered to Google Gemini. The app asks for your consent first. Stories and profiles are stored on your device and in your private iCloud, which we cannot access. Audio stays on the device. Settings → Delete all data removes your stories, profiles and anonymous account with one tap, including from iCloud. What is kept for a short time afterwards is explained in our privacy policy.
